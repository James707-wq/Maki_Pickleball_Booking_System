Terms & Policies

Booking rules, cancellation policy, payment terms, and service expectations.

Booking policy

  • Bookings must be made for an available time slot and start as pending and are confirmed only after the facility approves them.
  • Guests are responsible for arriving on time. Late arrivals may reduce available court time and no refund is guaranteed.
  • Only the booking holder may change, cancel, or confirm court details for a reservation.

Cancellation policy

  • Pending or confirmed bookings can be cancelled before the reserved start time.
  • Cancelled bookings may be refunded or credited according to facility rules and payment status.
  • Bookings cancelled after the court has started may be treated as completed or forfeited.

Payment policy

  • Payments are tracked as pending, paid, failed, or refunded.
  • Proof of payment may be requested before a customer is marked as paid by staff.
  • Any promotional code or discount is applied at the time the booking is created.

Terms of service

  • Customers agree to use the facility responsibly and comply with staff directions.
  • MAKI's PickleBall Court reserves the right to reject or cancel bookings that violate safety, fairness, or operating rules.
  • Facility notices, announcements, and support communication are considered part of the service experience.
